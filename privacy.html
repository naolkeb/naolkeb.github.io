Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This page covers the Channel Publisher app, a personal tool that uploads Na'ol's own videos to Na'ol's social media accounts.

What it collects

Nothing from anyone else. The only accounts the app signs in to are the owner's. From those, it receives only what it needs to upload a video: a sign-in token and basic account details, such as the account's display name.

Where it's kept

Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. They are never sent anywhere except back to the platform they came from, and they are never shared or sold.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from each platform's account settings, and can delete the locally stored tokens.
