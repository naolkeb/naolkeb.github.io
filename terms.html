This page covers the Channel Publisher app, a personal tool run by Na'ol. It uploads Na'ol's own videos to Na'ol's social media accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X).

Who can use it

The app is for the owner's own accounts only. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody else can sign up for it.

What it does

When the owner runs it, the app uploads videos and captions that the owner made, to accounts the owner has signed in with. It does not read, change or post to anyone else's account.
